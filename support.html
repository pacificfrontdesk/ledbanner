Common questions

How do I show the banner full screen?

Tap the banner preview at the top of the main screen, or the play button in the top right corner. Tap anywhere to exit. The screen stays awake while the banner is showing. Turn your device sideways for the biggest letters - if it does not rotate, turn off Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Center.

How do I save my banner as a video?

Tap the share button at the top of the main screen, then Create video. It makes a six-second clip you can send in a message or save to Photos. Create image makes a still instead. Free exports carry a small LED Banner mark in the corner; Pro removes it.

How do I use the Apple Watch app?

Open the Watch app on your iPhone, find LED Banner under Available Apps and install it. On the watch you can start or stop the full-screen banner, switch the message and change the effect. LED Banner needs to be open on your iPhone for the watch to reach it.

The eyes look squashed

Eyes mode has separate Width and Height sliders, so a low height with a wide width gives you a flat, angry-looking eye. Raise the height for a rounder one. The pair always keeps its shape when you turn the device; it just scales down to fit.

I bought Pro but the fonts are still locked

Tap the crown in the top left corner and then Restore Purchases. Pro is tied to your Apple Account, so make sure you are signed in with the same one you bought it with.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

How many presets can I save?

Three on the free version, and as many as you like with Pro. Presets are stored on your device, so deleting the app deletes them.

Why can't I change the text colour?

The Rainbow and Wave effects colour the text themselves, so the colour pickers are turned off while either one is on. Choose another effect, or None, to pick your own colours or a gradient.

My banner looks back to front

Mirror is switched on. It flips the text so it reads correctly in a mirror or from the other side of a window. Turn it off under Effects.
