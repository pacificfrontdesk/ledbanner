LED Banner

Scrolling Sign · for iPhone and iPad

Turn your screen into a glowing LED sign.

Type a message and LED Banner scrolls it across your screen in bright, glowing LED letters - for concerts, parties, airport pickups, sports games, shop windows, or just saying hello across a noisy room. Tap the banner and the whole screen becomes the sign.

Coming soon to the App Store

What you can do

  • Scroll left, right, up or down, or keep the text still, at any speed
  • Effects: Blink, Pulse, Rainbow, and Wave - a rainbow that flows through the letters
  • Gradient text that fades from one colour to another across your message
  • Any text colour and background, adjustable glow, square or round LED pixels
  • One-tap templates: Birthday, Welcome, Party, Congrats, Go Team, Open, Pickup and more
  • Mirror mode for showing a banner through a mirror or a car window
  • Full-screen mode that keeps the screen awake while your sign is up
  • Saved presets for the banners you use again and again

Free, with an optional Pro upgrade

Everything above is free. A one-time Pro purchase adds premium fonts and unlimited saved presets. No subscription, no adverts, no account.
