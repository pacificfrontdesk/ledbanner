LED Banner

Scrolling Sign · for iPhone and iPad

A glowing sign, or a pair of eyes staring back.

Type a message and LED Banner scrolls it across your screen in bright, glowing LED letters - for concerts, parties, airport pickups, sports games, shop windows, or just saying hello across a noisy room. Or switch to Eyes mode, and the screen becomes a pair of eyes that blink on their own and look around. Tap the banner and it fills the whole screen at full brightness.

Signs

  • Scroll left, right, up or down, or keep the text still, at any speed
  • Effects: Blink, Pulse, Rainbow, and Wave - a rainbow that flows through the letters
  • Gradient text that fades from one colour to another across your message
  • Any text colour and background, adjustable glow, square or round LED pixels
  • Stars, hearts, arrows and emoji you can tap straight into a message
  • 12 one-tap templates: Birthday, Welcome, Sale, Party, Congrats, Go Team, Open, Pickup, Wedding, Graduation, Taxi and Yard Sale
  • Mirror mode for showing a banner through a mirror or a car window
  • Full screen at full brightness, awake for as long as your sign is up
  • Saved banners you can bring back in a tap

Eyes

  • 21 styles: round, cat, sleepy, wide, wink, robot, heart and X eyes
  • An angry family of seven, from a mild scowl to a full glare
  • A Halloween set: Monster, Pumpkin, Skull, Spider and Zombie
  • Picked from a gallery of previews in your own colours, not from a list of names
  • Width and height set separately - wide and flat, or tall and narrow
  • Movement presets: slow blink, fast blink, sleepy, twitchy, look around, or a dead stare

Save it, or run it from your wrist

Export your banner as an image or a short video and send it to anyone. And with the Apple Watch app, start or stop the full-screen banner, switch the message and change the effect without touching your phone - handy when it is propped on a dashboard or a table.
