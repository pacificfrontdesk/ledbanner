Privacy Policy

LED Banner · last updated 11 September 2026

Who I am

LED Banner is published by Oleg Nochka, an individual developer ("I", "me"), who is responsible for the app and for this policy.

The short version

LED Banner does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Everything the app knows about you stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following locally, and nowhere else:

  • Your saved presets and the banner you were last editing
  • Your banner settings - text, font, colours, effects and speed
  • Whether you have purchased Pro

Deleting the app deletes all of it. I never see any of it.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. Payment details are never sent to me and I never see them. The app receives only a signal from Apple that the purchase is valid.

Children

LED Banner is rated 4+. Since it collects nothing, there is nothing to collect from a child either.

Changes

If this ever changes - if a future version collects anything at all - this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here.
